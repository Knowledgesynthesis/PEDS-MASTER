# **6. Pediatric Reasoning Framework**

Universal pediatric logic:
1. Determine child's age (critically affects differential).  
2. Identify red flags (fever < 28 days, stridor, cyanosis, lethargy).  
3. Stabilize and check hydration/respiratory status.  
4. Sort by pattern:
   - Respiratory  
   - Cardiac  
   - Infectious  
   - Developmental  
   - Nutritional  
5. Choose correct diagnostics (US, XR, viral panel, UA, echo conceptually).  
6. Apply evidence-based classification systems (asthma, CHD clues).  
7. Provide stepwise management (education-only).  
8. Plan follow-up and growth surveillance.

**Common pitfalls to address:**
- Assuming bronchodilators help bronchiolitis  
- Failing to differentiate AOM vs OME  
- Confusing PDA murmur with VSD  
- Not identifying epiglottitis early  
- Missing red flags in FTT  
- Overdiagnosing asthma in infants  
- Misinterpreting dehydration severity
